# Research

My research interests lie at the intersection of **statistics, machine learning, time-series analysis, and quantitative finance**.

## Areas of interest

- **Statistical learning and model interpretation:** understanding how models make predictions, assessing their performance, and identifying informative variables.
- **Time series and financial econometrics:** studying data that evolve over time and evaluating models with appropriate temporal structure.
- **Computational and quantitative finance:** using mathematical and statistical tools to investigate questions involving financial data.

## Current work

I am exploring interpretable, data-driven approaches to financial time-series problems. More detailed descriptions and reproducible materials will be added when the relevant work is ready for public release.

## 研究方向

目前關注統計學習、模型可解釋性、時間序列分析，以及金融資料的量化研究。這個頁面先整理研究興趣；尚未公開或尚未完成驗證的成果，不會提前列為正式研究發表。
